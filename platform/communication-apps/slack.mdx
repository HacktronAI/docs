## Prerequisites

You need to be a Hacktron organization admin to manage the Slack integration.

You also need permission to authorize apps in the Slack workspace you want Hacktron to use.

## Configure Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    In Hacktron, go to **Integrations** and find **Slack** under
    **Notifications**.

    <img src="/images/slack-integration-connect.png" alt="Slack under Notifications in Integrations, with a Connect button" />

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Slack">
    Click **Connect**, then **Connect Slack** in the panel that opens.
    Your browser redirects to Slack.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Hacktron">
    Sign in to Slack if required, choose the workspace, and approve the
    requested access.

    <Info>
      Hacktron uses this access to send security notifications and scan updates
      to your configured channel. It also lets people work with Hacktron by
      mentioning @Hacktron AI.
    </Info>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to Hacktron">
    After authorization, Hacktron redirects you back to **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Slack configuration">
    Click **Manage** next to Slack to open its panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a notification channel">
    Select the Slack channel that should receive Hacktron scan updates. The
    setting saves when you choose a different channel.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating the channel

Open the Slack panel again and choose a different channel. Hacktron saves the new destination for notifications.

## Disconnecting Slack

In **Integrations**, click **Manage** next to Slack, then **Disconnect Slack**. Confirm the disconnection to stop notifications and alerts.

## Common checks

- If Slack does not connect, retry from **Integrations**.
- If a channel is missing, confirm that the Slack workspace account used for OAuth can access that channel.
- If notifications are not appearing, confirm that the Slack integration is connected and a channel is selected.

## Next: use Hacktron in Slack

Connecting Slack installs the Hacktron AI app in your workspace. Add it to a channel with `/invite @Hacktron AI`. Each person then links their Slack and Hacktron accounts to use the assistant with their own permissions.
