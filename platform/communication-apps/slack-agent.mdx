title: "Ask Hacktron in Slack"
description: "Mention @Hacktron to ask about findings and set up pentests from Slack."
---

Mention **@Hacktron** in a channel, or send it a direct message, to ask about your scans and findings or to set up a pentest. It replies in a thread. Keep talking in that thread without mentioning it again. Teammates can join the thread by mentioning @Hacktron, and it acts for each person with their own access.

## Before you start

- An organization admin has [connected Slack](/platform/communication-apps/slack) to Hacktron.
- You've [linked your Slack account](/code-review/integrations/slack#link-your-slack-identity) to your Hacktron account. Hacktron acts as you, with your role: viewers can ask questions, members can set up pentests, and organization admins can start them.
- To mention Hacktron in a channel, add the Hacktron app to that channel first.

## Ask about findings

Ask in your own words, for example:

- "How many critical findings are open in acme/api?"
- "Show me the high severity findings from the last pentest"
- "Which repositories have the most open findings?"

Hacktron answers with counts and posts findings as cards that link to them in Hacktron.

## Set up a pentest

<Steps>
  <Step title="Say what to test">
    Name a connected repository, or attach a `.zip`, `.7z` or `.tar.gz` archive
    of the code (up to 2 GB) to your message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Narrow the scope">
    To test only part of the code, say so in the thread, for example "only the
    services/payments folder". There's no need to upload a smaller archive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a live site (optional)">
    Give Hacktron the URL of a deployed copy of the app to test it live as well
    as the code. A staging copy is the safest choice. Without one, the pentest
    reviews the code only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the cost">
    Hacktron estimates the pentest and asks you to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Hacktron posts an approval card. An organization admin clicks **Start
    pentest**. Credits are spent when the pentest starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get the results">
    When the pentest finishes, Hacktron posts the results in the same thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Test a site signed in

If the live site needs a login, Hacktron posts an **Add login details** button. It opens a private form that only you can see. The details go straight to Hacktron, are stored encrypted, and never appear in the thread or reach the assistant.

Hacktron then checks that it can sign in and posts the result in the thread. If the check fails, click **Update login details** to fix them.

A pentest tests its live sites either all signed in or all without a login. To test both kinds, set up a separate pentest for each.

<Note>
  Signed-in testing is enabled per organization. If it isn't available for
  yours, Hacktron says so and can still test the site without signing in.
</Note>

## Follow what it's doing

While it works, Hacktron shows its plan and each step it takes in a progress card at the top of its reply.

## Limits

- Hacktron reads attached archives to understand the code, but never runs your code. To test the running app, give it a deployed site.
- Each thread has a usage limit. When a thread reaches it, Hacktron stops and asks you to mention it in a new thread.
- If Hacktron can't read files you attach, ask an organization admin to reconnect Slack from **Integrations** so the app gets permission to read files.
