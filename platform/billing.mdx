Hacktron bills each product separately. Code Review runs on seats, with a 14-day free trial. Whitebox Scans and Automations use separate, organization-wide credit accounts.

## How billing works

| Product                                      | Billed by          | Pricing model |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------- |
| [Code Review](/code-review/billing)          | Developer seats    | Subscription  |
| [Whitebox Scans](/white-box-pentest/billing) | Scan credits       | Usage-based   |
| [Automations](/platform/automations/credits) | Automation credits | Usage-based   |

Organization owners manage billing. Other members cannot see the **Billing** page.

The Billing page shows the balance and current-period usage for each credit account. Owners can buy Automation credits or Whitebox Scan Credits independently. Each card also links to the relevant run or scan history, so spending from the two products does not get mixed together.

Auto top-up is available for Automation credits. Whitebox Scan Credits must be purchased manually.

## Code Review

Code Review is billed by seat. Each developer with Code Review enabled counts as one seat.

You are charged monthly for the seats active during that billing period. [Contact us](mailto:hello@hacktron.ai) for annual billing. Every organization starts with a 14-day free trial.

## Whitebox scans

Whitebox Scans use their own organization-wide credit account. The balance is not shared with Automations.

Calculate a scan's cost based on scope, and credits are deducted when the scan is started.

Automation credits are organization-wide. They are separate from your Whitebox Scan credits.
