Use **Personal settings** to manage your profile, connected accounts, and Hacktron account security. Open it from the account menu in the sidebar header.

## Profile

Your profile controls how you appear to the rest of your organization.

- **Display name**: the name shown on findings, comments, and activity.
- **Email**: the address used to sign in and receive account notifications.
- **Avatar**: the picture shown next to your name across the app.

## Connected accounts

Connect a source control account when Hacktron needs to associate your activity on that provider with your member account. Each provider appears separately under **Connected accounts**.

### Connect Microsoft for Azure DevOps

Linking your Microsoft account allows Hacktron to associate Azure DevOps pull requests with your Code Review seat and member profile. The email on the Microsoft account must match the pull request author's Azure DevOps user principal name (UPN).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Personal settings">
    Open the account menu in the sidebar header and select **Personal
    settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Microsoft">
    Under **Connected accounts**, find **Microsoft** and select **Connect**.
    Complete the identity check if Hacktron asks you to verify your account
    first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Microsoft sign-in">
    Sign in to the Microsoft account you use for Azure DevOps and approve the
    connection. Hacktron returns you to Personal settings and shows the linked
    email on the Microsoft row.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The connection does not rerun completed reviews. Future Azure DevOps pull request activity can reconcile an existing unlinked author entry once the matching Microsoft account is connected.

You can remove a connection with **Disconnect**. Disconnecting Microsoft can prevent later Azure DevOps activity from being attributed to your Hacktron member account.

## Multi-factor authentication (MFA)

Multi-factor authentication adds a second verification step to sign-in, so a password alone is not enough to access your account. We strongly recommend enabling MFA on every account.

### Enable MFA

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Personal settings">
    Open the account menu in the sidebar header and select **Personal settings**.

    ![Enable MFA from Personal settings](/images/mfa_enable.png)

  </Step>
  <Step title="Start MFA setup">
    Select **Enable MFA**. Hacktron shows a QR code for your authenticator app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Hacktron to your authenticator app">
    Scan the QR code with an authenticator app such as 1Password, Google Authenticator, or Authy. If you cannot scan it, enter the setup key manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the code">
    Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app to confirm setup. After it is verified, MFA is required the next time you sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Save your backup codes

When you enable MFA, Hacktron generates one-time backup codes. Store them in a safe place such as a password manager.

<Warning>
  Backup codes are the only way to sign in if you lose access to your
  authenticator app. Save them somewhere secure and do not share them.
</Warning>

### Disable or reset MFA

You can turn MFA off or run setup again from **Personal settings**. Disabling MFA weakens your account security, so only do this if you are switching to a new device or authenticator app.
