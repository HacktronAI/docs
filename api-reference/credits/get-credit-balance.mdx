---
title: "Get credit balance"
openapi: "GET /credits"
description: "Get the pentest credits available to your organization, with totals purchased, used, refunded and granted, and whether the organization is unlimited."
---

Returns the pentest credits available to the organization, with totals purchased, used, refunded and granted. `is_unlimited` is true for organizations without a credit limit.

<Info>**Scope required**: `read`</Info>
