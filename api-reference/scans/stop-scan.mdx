---
title: "Stop scan"
openapi: "POST /scans/{id}/stop"
description: "Stop a running scan or pentest and get its status. Members can stop their own pentests and any PR scan, and organization admins can stop any scan."
---

Stops a running scan and returns its status. Members can stop their own pentests and any PR scan. Organization admins can stop any scan.

<Info>**Scope required**: `write`. **Role**: member or above.</Info>
